Build Guide

STRStrengthRaises damage from component abilities and cup hits.
VITVitalityRaises maximum HP and your chance to shrug off a lost ball.
DEXDexterityAdds balls as it grows and increases critical-hit chance.
INTIntelligenceAmplifies component ability damage.
LCKLuckImproves gold rewards and bonus material drops.
Early build tip

Vitality gives you room to survive; Dexterity eventually adds another ball to every volley. Strength and Intelligence are your direct damage path, while Luck compounds your income.
