Early build tip

Vitality gives you room to survive; Dexterity eventually adds another ball to every volley. Strength and Intelligence are your direct damage path, while Luck compounds your income.

Machine rules

Positive components charge their own ability meter on impact. When full, the assigned effect fires. Wall springs and triangle springs add heat. A ball that overheats dies, costs 1 player HP, and returns to the tray.
